  • Base b → decimal: sum of digit × bposition (negative powers after the point).
  • Decimal → base b: integer part: successive ÷ b, read remainders bottom→top. Fraction: successive × b, read integer parts top→bottom.
  • 2 ↔ 8 ↔ 16: group bits by 3 (octal) or 4 (hex) from the point outward, padding with 0.
  • Binary addition: 0+0=0, 0+1=1, 1+1=10 (carry 1), 1+1+1=11. Subtraction: borrow, or add the 2's complement. Multiplication: shift and add. Division: shift and subtract.
  • Negative numbers: sign-magnitude (MSB = sign); 1's complement (invert bits); 2's complement (invert, +1). 2's range: −2n−1 … 2n−1−1. A−B = A + 2's comp(B); overflow when two operands of the same sign give a result of the opposite sign; discard the final carry.
  • Unknown base: write the polynomial, e.g. (111)b = b²+b+1 = 7, then solve for b.
  • Codes: BCD 8421 digit by digit; Excess-3 = BCD of (digit+3); Gray: gn=bn, gi=bi⊕bi+1; back: bn=gn, bi=bi+1⊕gi. ASCII on 7 bits: '0'=48, 'A'=65, 'a'=97.
  • IEEE 754: 32 bits = 1 sign + 8 exponent (bias 127) + 23 mantissa; 64 bits = 1 + 11 (bias 1023) + 52. Value = (−1)s × 1.m × 2E−bias. Steps: sign → |x| in binary → normalize → E = e + bias → mantissa.
  • Boolean algebra: A+A'=1, A.A'=0, A+AB=A, A+A'B=A+B; De Morgan: (AB)'=A'+B', (A+B)'=A'B'. Simplify algebraically, with a Karnaugh map (group 1, 2, 4, 8 adjacent 1s, Gray order) or Quine–McCluskey.
  • Adders: half adder S=A⊕B, C=A.B; full adder S=A⊕B⊕Cin, Cout=AB+Cin(A⊕B).
